L’EVENTO

Descrizione completa

Italy's most important international science fiction event, returns.

, the festival celebrates a special edition, featuring the best of internationalscience fiction and a packed calendar of events including cinema, literature, exhibitions, video games and new technologies, always striking the perfect balance between science and science fiction.

Again this year, the screens of the Politeama Rossetti and Teatro Miela will light up with the best fantastic film productions and new, surprising discoveries, in the presence of directors, actors and authors from all over the world. The spectacular Sci-Fi Dome will return to Piazza della Borsa, a specially designed structure that will host the festival's extra events: from Mondofuturo's science and science fiction outreach meetings to IVIPRO DAYS, an annual event

dedicated to video games as a tool for showcasing the region and its cultural heritage.

Ample space will also be reserved for literature, with a packed calendar of presentations and meetings and the second edition of the Mondofuturo Literary Prize, promoted by Area Science Park and La Cappella Underground.
