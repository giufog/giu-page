L’EVENTO

Descrizione completa

The Nativity scene tradition in Pagnacco is a journey through the displays created in the squares, hamlets, churches, and private homes. Even today, setting up the Nativity scene represents an occasion for celebration for many families in the community—a way to share stories around a common symbol. The inaugurations of the Nativity scenes are the highlight of the event, during which the festive hamlets come together to present their creations, the result of hard work and dedication.

Nativity scenes in churches: open according to the visiting hours of the places of worship
